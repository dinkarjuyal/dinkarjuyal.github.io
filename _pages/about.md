I am an ML Engineer at Meta working on agentic systems. Previously, I was a Staff ML Researcher and Engineer at PathAI, focused on foundation models and computer vision for digital pathology.

My work spans foundation models for pathology ([PLUTO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07905)), domain generalization ([ContriMix](https://wilds.stanford.edu/leaderboard/#camelyon17), best-performing official submission on the Camelyon17 WILDS leaderboard), interpretability ([Additive MIL](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/82764461a05e933cc2fd9d312e107d12-Paper-Conference.pdf), sparse-autoencoder [analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10785) of pathology embeddings), and evaluation of AI agents as ML engineers ([ML-Dev-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00964)). This work has been applied clinically, including [AI-based automation of enrollment criteria and endpoint assessment in liver disease trials](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03172-7) (Nature Medicine, 2024). See [publications](/publications/) for the full list.

I write about ML research — mostly notes on agents, foundation models, and evaluation. **New posts go up on [Substack](https://dinkarjuyal.substack.com/) first** (see below); some are later mirrored here.
